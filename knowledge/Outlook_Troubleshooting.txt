TITLE: Outlook Synchronization Troubleshooting
CATEGORY: Collaboration / Outlook
1. Confirm network connectivity.
2. Restart Outlook.
3. Check whether Outlook is in Offline mode.
4. Verify mailbox connectivity.
5. Restart the computer.
6. If synchronization remains stuck, recreate the Outlook profile using the approved enterprise procedure.
7. Escalate to Service Desk if unresolved.
